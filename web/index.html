Before a pilot

  1. Record the existing workflow and measured baseline.
  2. Check data access, residency and human-review gates.
  3. Agree accuracy, time and adoption success criteria.
  4. Validate benefits with finance and scope with presales.

Built to explain, not overclaim

What this project demonstrates

Hunt

A transparent account-fit score and BANT qualification. Every score shows its reasons; qualified stages require evidence fields.

Grow & retain

Existing-customer and renewal flags, a discovery brief and recorded follow-up actions. No automatic renewal or outreach.

Advise

Product-specific discovery questions and a cost-benefit scenario. AI is a solution category, not a claim that the app runs an AI model.

Own

Stage-weighted pipeline, explicit forecast window, overdue hygiene, closed-deal win rate, CSV/JSON exports and persistent SQLite records.

Model assumptions

Research 5% · Qualified 20% · Discovery 40% · Proposal 65% · Negotiation 80% · Won 100% · Lost 0%.

Fit score: employee size up to 25 + documented pain 25 + decision maker 20 + BANT completeness up to 30. This is a chosen heuristic, not proven conversion likelihood.

Timezones are region labels only. All values are manually normalized INR; no live FX or calendar integration. Demo date is 13 November 2026; change the snapshot date to explore later renewals.
